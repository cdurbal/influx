## Which laws apply

- As a company established in England, we are subject to the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
- If you live in the European Union or the European Economic Area, the EU General Data Protection Regulation also applies, because we offer our services to you.
- We apply the protection described in this policy to every user, wherever they live. The law of your country may give you additional rights, for example in Brazil, in Quebec or in some American states; we respect them.

## Data collected

- At sign-up: e-mail address, display name, country, preferred language, declared status, and for a brand the company name.
- For a creator: the statement of being an adult, and its date.
- Your acceptances of the terms of service and your consent choices, with their date, the language and the text shown.
- How you heard about INFLUX, if you tell us.
- Technical logs needed to keep the service secure, limited to what is strictly necessary.

This version covers sign-up and the account. Before creator profiles, campaigns and messaging are opened, this policy will be updated to describe the corresponding data.

## Why we use your data, and on which legal basis

- Creating and managing your account, and signing you in with a code sent by e-mail: performance of the contract formed by the terms of service.
- Organising connections between brands and creators: performance of that contract.
- Keeping the service secure and preventing fraud: our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users.
- Keeping a record of your acceptances of the terms and of your consent choices: our obligation to be able to demonstrate consent, and our legitimate interest in proving what was accepted and in establishing or defending legal claims.
- Understanding how you heard about INFLUX: our legitimate interest in knowing which channels bring us new members. Answering is optional.
- Recording your contact details in Brevo and Apollo.io, to send you marketing e-mails about INFLUX and for sales prospecting: your consent.

## Your marketing consent

At this stage of the service, the box by which you agree to your contact details being recorded in Brevo and Apollo.io, for marketing e-mails and for sales prospecting, is required to create an account. We tell you so openly.

- You can withdraw this agreement at any time, in one action from your account, without giving a reason.
- Withdrawal takes effect immediately at INFLUX and is then passed on to Brevo and Apollo.io.
- Withdrawal does not close your account and does not stop the e-mails needed for the service, such as sign-in codes.
- Every marketing e-mail also lets you unsubscribe.

This way of working is being reviewed by a solicitor and may change.

- **Brevo**, established in the European Union, sends the e-mails of the service (sign-in codes, messages about your account) and, with your agreement, communications about INFLUX. Brevo receives your e-mail address, language, country and account type.
- **Apollo.io**, established in the United States, may be used for the sales follow-up of people who sign up, with your agreement, and only in countries where this use rests on a validated legal basis. Apollo.io then receives sign-up information limited to that purpose.
- **Supabase** (database) and **Railway** (application hosting) host the service. Hosting region: [HOSTING REGION].
- INFLUX staff, in particular the sales agents and administrators, only to the extent needed for their tasks.
- Other users: brands see creator profiles in anonymised form. Identities are revealed to the two parties only once both have accepted a connection. Your e-mail address and phone number are never shared with another user.
- Public authorities, where the law requires it.

- For transfers to the United States, in particular to Apollo.io, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, to which Apollo.io states that it adheres, or failing that on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission with the UK Addendum, or on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

## Your rights

- Access your data and obtain a copy of it.
- Have it corrected.
- Ask for it to be erased; the erasure is also passed on to Brevo and Apollo.io. Some data may be kept where the law requires it, such as contract and invoicing records.
- Object to processing, or ask for it to be restricted. You can object to direct marketing at any time, without giving a reason.
- Receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format, and have it passed on to another organisation.
- Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise these rights, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within one month; this period may be extended by two months for complex or numerous requests, in which case we tell you why.

No decision based solely on automated processing and producing legal or similarly significant effects on you is taken: every connection is reviewed by an INFLUX sales agent.

## Complaints

- You can complain to us first at [CONTACT EMAIL]. We acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and handle it without undue delay.
- You can also complain at any time to the Information Commission (ICO) in the United Kingdom, to the data protection authority of the country of the European Union where you live, work or where the issue arose, or to the competent authority of your country.

## Minors

INFLUX is for adults only. No account is opened for a person under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we close it and delete the related data, subject to what the law requires us to keep.
