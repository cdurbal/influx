# INFLUX Privacy Policy

This text is a working draft. The final version will be reviewed by a lawyer before any public launch; it may differ significantly from this draft.

## Data controller

Data is processed by the operator of the INFLUX platform. Its full identity and contact details will be stated in the final version.

## Data collected

- At sign-up: e-mail address, display name, country, preferred language, declared status, and for a brand the company name.
- For a creator: the statement of being an adult, and its date.
- Your acceptances of the terms of service and your consent choices, with their date and the text shown.
- How you heard about INFLUX, if you tell us.
- Technical logs needed to keep the service secure, limited to what is strictly necessary.

## Purposes

- Creating and managing your account, and signing you in with a code sent by e-mail.
- Organising connections between brands and creators.
- Keeping the service secure and preventing fraud.
- Sending you communications about INFLUX, with your agreement.

## Brevo

INFLUX uses **Brevo** to send the e-mails of the service (sign-in codes, messages about your account) and, with your agreement, communications about INFLUX. Brevo receives your e-mail address, language, country and account type.

## Apollo.io

INFLUX may use **Apollo.io** for the sales follow-up of people who sign up, with your agreement, and only in countries where this use rests on a validated legal basis. Apollo.io then receives sign-up information limited to that purpose.

## Hosting

The application and the database are hosted by technical providers. Their location, and the safeguards for any transfer outside your country, will be stated in the final version.

## Retention

How long each category of data is kept will be stated in the final version.

## Your rights

- Access your data and obtain a copy of it.
- Have it corrected.
- Ask for it to be erased; the erasure is also passed on to Brevo and Apollo.io.
- Object to processing, or ask for it to be restricted.
- Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
- Lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

How to exercise these rights will be stated in the final version.

## Minors

INFLUX is for adults only. No account is opened for a minor.
