# INFLUX Cookie Policy

This text is a working draft. The final version will be reviewed by a lawyer before any public launch; it may differ significantly from this draft.

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. It can, for example, keep you signed in from one page to the next.

## Strictly necessary cookies

INFLUX uses cookies that the service cannot work without:

- a sign-in journey cookie, while you enter the code received by e-mail;
- a sign-up journey cookie, while you finish signing up;
- a session cookie, which keeps you signed in and expires after a period of inactivity.

These cookies cannot be read by the page's scripts and are never used for advertising. They do not require your consent.

## Remembering your choice

Your choice on the cookie banner may be remembered so that you are not asked again on every visit.

## Analytics or advertising cookies

INFLUX does not currently set any advertising cookie. If analytics or advertising cookies were added, they would only be set with your agreement, and this policy would be updated.

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies from your browser settings. Deleting the session cookie signs you out.
