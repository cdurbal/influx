## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. It can, for example, keep you signed in from one page to the next. This policy also covers similar technologies that store or read information on your device.

## The rules that apply

- In the United Kingdom, storing or reading information on your device is governed by regulation 6 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. Your consent is required, except for cookies that are strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for, and in the other limited cases provided for by law.
- If you live in the European Union, the equivalent rules of your country apply. They also exempt strictly necessary cookies from consent.

## Strictly necessary cookies

INFLUX uses cookies that the service cannot work without:

- a sign-in journey cookie, while you enter the code received by e-mail;
- a sign-up journey cookie, while you finish signing up;
- a session cookie, which keeps you signed in and expires after a period of inactivity.

These cookies cannot be read by the page's scripts and are never used for advertising. Because they are strictly necessary, they do not require your consent.

## Remembering your choice

Your choice on the cookie banner may be remembered so that you are not asked again on every visit.

## Analytics or advertising cookies

INFLUX does not currently set any advertising cookie. If analytics or advertising cookies were added, they would only be set with your prior agreement, including where UK law would allow some statistical cookies to be set without consent, and this policy would be updated beforehand. Refusing would be as simple as accepting.

## Managing cookies

You can delete cookies from your browser settings. Deleting the session cookie signs you out.

## Personal data

Where a cookie involves personal data, it is processed as described in the privacy policy, which can be read without signing in.
