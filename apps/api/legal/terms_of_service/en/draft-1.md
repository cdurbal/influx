# INFLUX Terms of Service

This text is a working draft. It binds neither INFLUX nor its users. The final version will be reviewed by a lawyer before any public launch; it may differ significantly from this draft.

## Purpose

INFLUX is a platform that connects brands (advertisers) with content creators (influencers). These terms describe the rules for using the platform.

The full identity of the platform operator will be stated in the final version.

## Sign-up and account

- Signing up is free.
- The platform is open only to people who are adults under the law of their country.
- A brand signs up on behalf of a company or an organisation.
- You sign in with a one-time code sent by e-mail. You are responsible for access to your e-mail address.
- The information you provide must be accurate and kept up to date.

## How connections work

- Brands can browse creator profiles shown anonymously.
- Creators can browse campaigns and apply to them.
- No conversation opens without the approval of an INFLUX sales agent, followed by the acceptance of both parties.
- The INFLUX sales agent is a named, visible intermediary in every conversation.
- The platform never shares the direct contact details of the parties (e-mail address, phone number).

## How INFLUX is paid

INFLUX is paid through a commission on the contracts made through the platform. During the pilot phase, the amount of each contract, commission included, is stated by your INFLUX sales agent before anything is signed.

## Draft non-circumvention clause

**This clause is a draft, subject to review by a lawyer.**

Users would undertake not to make a contract directly, outside the platform, with a party introduced to them by INFLUX, for a period to be stated in the final version. Exchanges and contracts arising from a connection would remain traceable within INFLUX.

## Content and conduct

- Each user remains responsible for the content they publish on the platform.
- Unlawful, misleading or hateful content, and content that infringes the rights of others, is forbidden.
- INFLUX may suspend an account that breaks these rules, after informing the person concerned where possible.

## Personal data

How personal data is processed is described in the privacy policy, which can be read without signing in.

## Changes to these terms

A new version of these terms may be published. It will be shown to you the next time you sign in and must be accepted to keep using the service. Declining does not delete your account.

## Governing law

The governing law and the competent courts will be stated in the final version.
